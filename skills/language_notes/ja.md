# Language Notes: Japanese (JA)

## Keigo (honorific verb form) gate
When Jesus (イエス) or God (神) is the subject, the verb must be in honorific form
(敬語), not plain form. Other characters (Mary, Peter, etc.) stay in plain form —
that's correct, don't flag it. Requires knowing who the grammatical subject of each
sentence is — not mechanically checkable by pattern-matching.
