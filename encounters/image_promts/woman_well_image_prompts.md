## STYLE NOTES FOR ALL CARDS
- Painterly warm 2D illustration — same visual language as Bartimaeus series
- Mediterranean palette: ochre, sandstone, burnt sienna, deep shadow, warm gold
- No photorealism — illustrated, painterly, cinematic
- No halos, no supernatural glow effects
- The woman must feel like the SAME person across all 12 cards
- When Jesus appears (cards 2, 6, 7) — soft focus or partial silhouette. The story is hers.
- Jesus is never the visual center of any card
